A forest of real photographs.

The footer is a photographic collage, assembled from the seven images credited below. It contains five flowering species: Fritillaria meleagris, Tricyrtis hirta, Passiflora incarnata, Eryngium alpinum, and Helleborus foetidus.

Flowers, leaves and branches were cropped and masked by hand, resized, mirrored, rotated, layered over photographs of forest and sky, and color graded. A deterministic displacement and light blur create the glass effect. No AI-generated imagery or generative editing was used. This is an imaginative montage, not a record of these plants growing together.
